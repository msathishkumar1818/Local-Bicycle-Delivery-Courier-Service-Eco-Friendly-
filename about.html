Our Journey

From A Single Garage to Citywide Infrastructure

Key milestones that shaped our zero-emission logistics network into the most reliable bicycle delivery service in the metropolitan district.

2021

Garage Genesis

Launched with 5 couriers and a handheld walkie-talkie dispatch system, serving local bakeries and law practices within a 3-kilometer radius.

2023

E-Cargo Expansion

Introduced our first custom-built 200kg heavy-duty electric cargo tricycles, enabling freight and bulk retail logistics at cycling velocity.

2025

Micro-Hub Network

Established 4 automated cross-docking stations across metro quadrants, cutting average delivery times from 45 minutes to under 24 minutes.

2026

Verified ESG Standard

Surpassed 140,000kg of displaced CO2, launching real-time API integrations and digital carbon accounting ledgers for business clients.
